# SafeRoute — Pre-Seed/Seed Investor Target List

Compiled 2026-08-11 from primary-source web research (press releases, fund sites, funding
announcements). Each entry notes *why* it fits. Items marked **[verify]** came from
aggregator databases or conflicting sources — confirm before outreach.

**Positioning insight from the research:** pure "school safety" companies (Raptor,
CENTEGIX, Navigate360, Gaggle, Omnilert) were overwhelmingly bootstrapped or PE-funded —
there is no deep pre-seed VC pool under that label. The venture money in this space
flows to **video-AI / physical-security AI** (Ambient.ai, Coram, Verkada, Flock, Hakimo)
and **emergency-response software** (RapidSOS, Prepared). Pitch SafeRoute as
**"AI on existing cameras + real-time emergency response, with K-12 as the wedge"** —
not as school-safety hardware.
